•    Project description

The software is the result of development carried out as part of an R&D multiprogram ( Project ID: GINOP_PLUSZ-1.1.2-21-2022-00043). The aim of this project was to create a centre of operations of STS Service Ltd. which will facilitate the improvement of its services, including its quality and efficiency. In relation to the site, an installation base was created where STS is able to install our intelligent monitoring systems in our projects, pre-equipped and prepared by ourselves.

With the realisation of our site, STS also aims to have their own product portfolio. STS would also like to make the site they are developing a centre for R&D activities, which will provide them with the opportunity to realise further potential developments.

 

As part of the complex site development, the storage of the industrial hall will be powered exclusively by renewable energy. An intelligent energy management system and monitoring centre was installed, which is able to monitor the solar power plants in real time. A high-power drone was installed on a remotely controlled programmed trajectory on site for automatic fault location inspection.

 

•    Duration

The development took place in 2024.

•    Dissemination objective

The purpose of open source publication is to make the development results available to the professional community.
